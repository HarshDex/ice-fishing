# Real presenter for Mira

Put presenter media in this folder and the game uses it automatically instead of the painted portrait (reload the page after adding files). If nothing is here, the painted portrait stays.

## Best: two short video clips

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `idle.mp4` (or `idle.webm`) | The presenter listening: looking at the camera, a small smile, blinking, slight natural movement. |
| `talk.mp4` (or `talk.webm`) | The same presenter talking naturally to the camera (any words; no sound is used). |

The game cross-fades to `talk` whenever Mira speaks and back to `idle` when she stops. Her subtitles and voice still come from the game, so the words always match what is happening.

For a seamless switch:

- Same person, outfit, framing, lighting and background in both clips. Film both in one session without moving the camera.
- Portrait 3:4 framing (for example 720 × 960), head and shoulders, face in the upper third.
- 4–8 seconds each, cut so the end flows into the start (it loops).
- MP4 (H.264) or WebM, no audio track needed, ideally under 4 MB each.

Lip movement will not match each word exactly. It looks like a presenter talking, which is how most live-show games do it.

## Simpler: one photo

`mira.jpg` (or `.png` / `.webp`), portrait 3:4, head and shoulders. The game adds gentle breathing, head tilts and nods. The mouth does not move.

## Where to get the media

- Film yourself or a presenter who has agreed to it, or
- generate a presenter with an AI video or image tool whose licence allows you to use the result in your game.

Only use footage or photos you own or have permission to use. Do not use pictures of real people taken from the internet.
